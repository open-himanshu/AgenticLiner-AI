## 1. System Overview & Architecture
AgenticLiner is an autonomous, AI-driven event planning and management platform designed specifically for university ecosystems (academic departments, Dean offices, and student societies like Technojam, IEEE, and Rotaract).

Unlike generic event software that relies on commercial billing or ticket selling, AgenticLiner manages **Campus Facility Requisitions, Logistics Stock Quotas, Academic Timetabling, and Faculty/Dean Approvals** while ensuring **zero state pollution** through its built-in safety engine (**Kavach**).

## 2. Navigating the 17 Screens & Workflows

### Role 1: Event Organizers (Clubs, Faculty & Coordinators)

#### Screen #1: AI Event Planning Copilot (`/`)
- **Purpose**: Input natural language event briefs (e.g., *"Organize a 2-day AI Hackathon for 150 students with coding workshops and cordless mics"*).
- **Features**:
  - **Adaptive Intake Engine**: Automatically extracts headcount, timeline, and nature of the event. If key details are missing, it asks targeted clarification questions before reserving resources.
  - **Countdown Task Board**: Automatically generates milestones for D-7, D-3, D-1, and D-Day with specific club roles.
  - **Campus Logistics Requisition**: Direct requisition of campus equipment (microphones, extension cords, JBL audio systems, student lunch quotas).

#### Screen #2: Autopilot Mode (`/autopilot`)
- **Purpose**: Autonomous multi-step event execution.
- **Features**:
  - Live execution timeline showing step-by-step tool invocation.
  - Visual status pill: shows active SQLite `SAVEPOINT` status (`COMMITTED` in green, or `ROLLED_BACK` in amber with safety reasoning).

#### Screen #3: Timetable & Schedule (`/schedule`)
- **Purpose**: Conflict-free session grid generated by the **Google OR-Tools CP-SAT Solver**.
- **Features**:
  - Displays all sessions, room assignments, allocated speakers, and time slots.
  - **Hero Demo: Speaker Cancels**: Simulates an unexpected guest speaker cancellation; dynamically prompts the scheduler to re-solve or reassign the slot.

#### Screen #4: Printable Event Flyer & Poster (`/flyer`)
- **Purpose**: Official campus event poster ready for physical printing or digital sharing.
- **Features**:
  - Displays event headline, venue, dates, agenda highlights.
  - Includes a generated high-resolution QR code directing students to the registration portal.

#### Screen #5: Attendee & Registration Management (`/attendees`)
- **Purpose**: Real-time management of student registrations and attendance.
- **Features**:
  - Roster table with search and department filtering.
  - Badges showing `Confirmed` or `Waitlisted`.
  - **Gate Check-In Button**: One-click physical check-in marking attendance.
  - **Auto-Promotion Engine**: When a confirmed student cancels, the system automatically promotes the next waitlisted student and stages a notification.
  - **CSV Export**: One-click download of the complete roster (`/api/attendees/export`).

#### Screen #6: Transactional Outbox & Broadcast Center (`/notices`)
- **Purpose**: Campus notification management implementing the **Transactional Outbox Pattern**.
- **Features**:
  - Shows staged notices (e.g. Schedule Updates, Waitlist Promotions, Urgent Room Changes).
  - Guarantees that announcements are only released if the underlying database transaction commits.
  - Manual broadcast composer for faculty announcements.

#### Screen #7: Post-Event Analytics & Verifiable Certificates (`/analytics`)
- **Purpose**: Post-event feedback intelligence and official credential issuance.
- **Features**:
  - Sentiment analysis scorecards (Positive, Neutral, Negative feedback ratios).
  - Thematic strengths & concerns synthesis across Venue, Content, Hospitality, and Organization.
  - **Download Executive Summary Report**: Generates an executive PDF report for the Dean (`/api/reports/executive-summary`).
  - **Certificate Verification Desk**: Allows faculty or employers to enter a Certificate ID or scan a QR code to verify authenticity against SHA-256 signatures.

### Role 2: Student Participants & Attendees

#### Screen #8: Public Student Registration Form (`/register`)
- **Purpose**: Student signup portal for campus events.
- **Features**:
  - Clean registration interface with real-time capacity checks.
  - If seats are filled, automatically places the student into the priority waitlist.

#### Screen #9: Personalized Participant Experience (`/participant`)
- **Purpose**: Individual attendee dashboard.
- **Features**:
  - **TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity Recommender**: Recommends personalized sessions matching the student's declared interests (e.g., AI/ML, Web Dev, Cybersecurity) with automatic clash-pruning.
  - **Peer & Faculty Mentor Matching**: Discovers fellow participants and faculty mentors with overlapping interests.
  - **Grounded Event Q&A Assistant**: In-page campus chatbot that queries the live database to answer questions about venues, timings, food counters, and Wi-Fi credentials.

## 3. Emergency Speaker-Cancellation Workflow (Hero Demo)
1. Navigate to `/schedule`.
2. Locate the **Hero Demo: Speaker Cancels** button on the top-right toolbar.
3. Click the button to trigger an emergency faculty cancellation simulation.
4. The system executes the CP-SAT solver, re-allocates the session or marks the slot with an available faculty co-speaker, and stages an Outbox broadcast to notify all registered students.
